What is this?

A free addon that pulls live ElevateX network flights and injects them into MSFS 2024 as AI aircraft with proper FSLTL airline liveries. Watch real pilots fly around you in real time.

Requirements

  • Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024
  • FSLTL Traffic Base installed in your Community folder

How to install

  1. Download the zip above
  2. Extract it anywhere
  3. Start MSFS 2024 and load your flight
  4. Run ElevateXTrafficInjector.exe as admin
  5. Enter your ElevateX username (optional — excludes your own flights)
  6. Press START

How it works

Every 12 seconds the injector downloads the ElevateX live feed, picks the flights nearest to you, matches each one to an FSLTL model and airline livery, and spawns them via SimConnect. Between feed updates it interpolates smooth motion so aircraft glide continuously instead of teleporting.

Known bugs (V1)

Takeoff surge — aircraft on the runway can suddenly accelerate then settle repeatedly instead of rolling smoothly.
Pitch attitude — nose-up/down during climbs and descents is not always correct yet.
