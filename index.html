What is this?

Watch real pilots fly around you.

This free addon pulls live ELEVATEX network flights and injects them into MSFS 2024 as AI aircraft. Each one gets a proper FSLTL model and airline livery, moving smoothly in real time based on actual pilot positions.

Requirements

What you need.

  • Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024
  • FSLTL Traffic Base (via the FlyByWire Installer) installed in your Community folder
Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download the zip above
  2. Extract it anywhere
  3. Start MSFS 2024 and load your flight
  4. Run ElevateXTrafficInjector.exe as admin
  5. Enter your ELEVATEX username (optional — excludes your own flights)
  6. Press START
How it works

Every flight, in real time.

The ELEVATEX network tracks every member''s flight as they fly — position, altitude, speed, heading, all updating live. This injector taps into that feed and brings those exact flights into your sim as AI aircraft.

Every 12 seconds it pulls the latest positions, figures out which flights are closest to you, and matches each one to the correct FSLTL aircraft model and airline livery. A British Airways A320 piloted by someone on the network shows up as an actual BA A320 — not a generic grey box.

Between feed updates, the injector interpolates smooth motion along a curve, so aircraft glide through the sky instead of jumping from point to point. Climbs, descents, turns, takeoffs and landings all follow the real pilot''s flight path. It''s the closest thing to sharing the sky with the ELEVATEX community.

Known bugs

What we're fixing.

Takeoff surge — aircraft on the runway can suddenly accelerate then settle repeatedly instead of rolling smoothly.
Pitch attitude — nose-up/down during climbs and descents is not always correct yet.

Found a bug we missed? Feel free to report it on GitHub — we'll fix it as soon as possible! Thank you!
